About us

A partner for building owners, from empty floors to full occupancy.

Founded in 2004 E.C., Marena works with owners of commercial, office and apartment buildings. We study the market, plan the right mix of tenants for each floor, set rents, and bring in the tenants and buyers the building needs.

Once the building is occupied we stay on to run it: collecting rent, looking after security, cleaning and maintenance, and keeping the owner informed.

What we do

Four services for commercial, office and apartment buildings

Take one service or all four. Most owners start with organising and stay with us for management.

01

Organising

Market study, a floor-by-floor tenant mix, rent pricing and a marketing strategy, ready before the building opens.

02

Leasing and sales

Finding suitable tenants and buyers, negotiating terms and preparing agreements on the owner's behalf.

03

Consulting

Advice for owners and developers on layout, pricing and how to get more from an existing building.

04

Management

Rent collection, security, cleaning, parking, maintenance and utilities, with regular reports to the owner.
