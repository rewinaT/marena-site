About us

Organising, leasing, selling, managing and advising, in whole or in part.

Marena Property Solution works on commercial centres, office buildings, mixed-use buildings and residential apartments. We organise, lease, sell, manage and advise, for the whole building or part of it, according to what each owner asks for.

Our professionals have served more than 45 small and large commercial, office and residential apartment buildings. We also carry out feasibility studies, market surveys, building management training and staff recruitment.

Interior designers, marketing agencies, IT and communication, technical and maintenance, and research and survey professionals work with us as partners.

Our services

Four services for commercial, office and apartment buildings

We work under a signed agreement with the owner, with honesty, transparency and competence.

01

Organising

  • Survey local rents and propose a rent that is profitable and acceptable to tenants.
  • Study the mix of business types that keeps the building let over the long term.
  • Set up security, cleaning, maintenance and parking units, with the staff or agencies to run them.
  • Prepare the management structure, HR manual, salary survey and working forms.
02

Leasing and sales

  • Assign leasing staff to register tenants, show the spaces, follow up and sign contracts with the owner.
  • Design a results-focused marketing strategy and give sales training.
  • Bring qualified, ready tenants and buyers gathered by our firm.
  • Negotiate in the building's long-term interest and present the terms to the owners for decision.
03

Consulting

  • Advise on how the building can create better rental and sales value.
  • Point to profitable practices that recover the cost of the building sooner.
  • Help set up modern, efficient building management and administration.
  • Plan and coordinate advertising and promotion.
04

Management

  • Take over marketing, administration, part of finance, security and parking, cleaning and technical work, and hire the staff for it.
  • Check that services are delivered 24/7 with the staffing they need.
  • Check daily that the building's rules on parking, waste disposal and goods movement are kept.
  • Oversee preventive maintenance of lifts, electrical boards, water tanks and generators.
